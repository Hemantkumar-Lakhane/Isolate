- `product_api_usage` — Returns customer accounts with usage metrics, DAU trends, support data, renewal dates, and health labels
- `product_api_nps` — Returns NPS scores and verbatim feedback

## DATA GATHERING STEPS
1. Call `product_api_usage` with default parameters to get the account sample
2. Call `product_api_nps` with `score_below=7` to get at-risk NPS data
3. Compile all data into the output JSON below

- `company_name` → account name
- `mrr` → monthly recurring revenue

- `renewal_date` → contract renewal date
- `_health_label` → pre-computed health indicator ("critical"/"at_risk"/"healthy")
